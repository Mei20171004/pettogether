User Privacy Choices

Here is how to review, change, or request removal of information connected with your PetTogether account.

Updated: September 28, 2026

Edit or remove pet care information

Use the app to update your family profile, pet details, tasks, medication plans, and health records. You can delete supported records from their detail screens. Changes to shared household information may also affect other members.

Manage household sharing

Review the people in your household in the app's Family area. Household owners can remove members; members can leave a household. Information shared with a household can be seen by its authorized members while they have access.

Control notifications and device permissions

Turn care notifications on or off in PetTogether's Family settings. On iPhone, open Settings → Apps → PetTogether to manage notifications, camera, photos, microphone, and speech recognition permissions. Device settings and labels may vary by iOS version.

Request access, correction, or account deletion

  1. Email [PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL — replace before publishing].
  2. State whether you want a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion of your account and associated data.
  3. Include the email address used for your PetTogether account so we can locate it. We may ask you to verify ownership before acting.

If a household contains other members, some shared information may need to remain available to them or be handled with the household owner. We will explain any such limitation when responding to your request.

Manage subscriptions

Changing privacy settings or deleting an account does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription. On iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions to manage or cancel it.

About these choices

Signing out or uninstalling PetTogether does not delete your cloud account or shared household data. Read our Privacy Policy for details about data use, sharing, and retention.
